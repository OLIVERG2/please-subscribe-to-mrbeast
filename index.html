THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

SYS_REF: ISS-COLUMBUS
"In microgravity, standard static packaging becomes space waste. Kinetic origami transforms structural dead weight into functional payload."

Space logistics are severely constrained by launch fairing volume and payload envelope boundaries. Traditional cargo storage utilizes rigid metallic containers that offer zero volume adaptability post-unpacking, consuming critical habitable volume aboard the International Space Station. Our solution introduces a dynamic origami-inspired system that collapses down to 15.8% of its original size.

> PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY + ZERO PINCH POINTS + ZERO OFF-GASSING

REQUIREMENT & SPEC PROTOTYPE SOLUTION ENGINEERING REASONING & JUSTIFICATION
// SPEC_01 - VOLUMETRICS
Compact Stowing Ratio

Target: >80% volume reduction during launch transit.
Modified Miura-Ori Pattern

Rigid facet panels connected by elastomeric compliant flexures.
Engineering Rationale: Traditional hinges add mechanical point-failures and weight. By utilizing a continuous compliant origami tessellation, we achieved an 84.2% volume reduction while ensuring single-degree-of-freedom deployment, preventing binding in microgravity.
// SPEC_02 - HUMAN FACTORS
Zero-G EVA Operability

Must be operable using bulky Extravehicular Mobility Unit (EMU) gloves.
Tactile Oversized Quick-Tabs

High-contrast yellow pull-rings with magnetic self-indexing locks.
Engineering Rationale: Pressurized gloves restrict dexterity and eliminate fine tactile feel. Standard latches cause crew fatigue. Our magnetic quick-tabs auto-align upon approach and require only 1.2 Nm of force to actuate, eliminating pinch hazards completely.
// SPEC_03 - MATERIAL SCIENCE
Thermal & Off-Gassing Integrity

NASA MSFC-SPEC-522 compliance for cabin toxic off-gassing.
PEEK Substructure + Fluoropolymer Joints

Polyether ether ketone panels with UV-stabilized fluoropolymer hinge layers.
Engineering Rationale: Silicone hinges degraded under vacuum thermal cycling in early prototypes. PEEK provides extreme stiffness-to-weight ratio, while aerospace fluoropolymer living hinges survive -120°C to +150°C without embrittlement or toxic off-gassing.
