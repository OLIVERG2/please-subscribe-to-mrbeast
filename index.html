01 // MISSION CHALLENGE

THE PROBLEM

SYS_REF: ISS-LOGISTICS
Traditional space cargo packaging relies heavily on rigid, single-use structures that consume excessive payload volume during launch and transition into unusable orbital waste. Standard mechanical hinges create severe pinch hazards for crew members in microgravity and pose significant risks of cold-welding in orbital vacuum conditions. Without adaptable, collapsible designs, space missions sacrifice valuable volume, efficiency, and operational safety.

BUILD PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES
01

PRIMARY PROTOTYPE OBJECTIVE

The physical prototype was constructed as a tangible proof of concept to validate kinetic origami flexure mechanics beyond digital CAD simulations. Building a 1:1 scale physical model allowed our engineering team to directly measure maximum volumetric stowage compression during transit, evaluate deployment latch forces, and verify zero-pinch hinge operation for safe astronaut manipulation during spaceflight missions.
